# Product Requirements Document (PRD) — Ordra

## Vision

Ordra is the intelligent operating system for modern cafes — combining operations, ordering, billing, analytics, and AI advisory in one multi-tenant SaaS.

## Tagline

**Ordra — Intelligent operations for modern cafes.**

## Problem

Cafe owners juggle POS, Excel, WhatsApp orders, kitchen chaos, and gut-feel decisions. Tools are fragmented; insights arrive too late.

## Target users (v1)

- Cafe owners / managers
- Floor staff & kitchen staff
- Diners (QR ordering)

## Goals (v1)

1. Digitize cafe operations end-to-end for a single cafe tenant
2. Enforce secure multi-tenancy from day one
3. Deliver actionable analytics and an AI advisor path
4. Support subscription monetization

## Non-goals (foundation phase)

Implementing auth UI, menus, orders, payments, or AI features.

## Success metrics (later)

- Time-to-first-order after onboarding
- Orders per active cafe
- Retention / paid conversion
- AI feature adoption
